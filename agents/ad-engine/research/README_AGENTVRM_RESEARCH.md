## 5 Critical Success Factors (Don't Skip)

1. **Hook in first 3 seconds** - Non-negotiable. 90% bounce if not engaged.
2. **Show actual product** - Screen recording or real interface (builds trust).
3. **Include specific testimonial** - Real person, real number (reduces risk perception).
4. **State offer clearly** - "Free 7 days, No CC, Limited slots" (drives urgency).
5. **Use captions** - 60-70% watch muted (critical for sound-off experience).
