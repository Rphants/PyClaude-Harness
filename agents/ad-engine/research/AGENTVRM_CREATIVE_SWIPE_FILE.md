## FINAL NOTES

**Remember:**
1. Every hook must establish pain in first 2 seconds
2. Every script includes specific numbers (trust builder)
3. Every ad has clear CTA (free trial, limited time)
4. Every visual has emotional element (relief, success, clarity)
5. Every testimonial is from real agent with real result

**Test in this order:**
- Week 1-2: Money angle vs. Time angle vs. Pain angle
- Week 3-4: Testimonial format vs. Demo format
- Week 5-6: Carousel vs. Video vs. Static
- Week 7+: Scaled production of winners

**Winning pattern typically:**
- Money angle hook
- AI demo in middle
- Real testimonial with number
- Free trial CTA with scarcity
