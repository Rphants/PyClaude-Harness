### Audio Quality Insights (from Ronald + team testing)
- Luis Uribe heard a sample and said "dammmmmmmn that actually sounds very good"

- Short sentences, contractions, `...` pauses make it sound natural
- Motivated tone outperforms flat/professional tone for wholesale callbacks
- eleven_turbo_v2_5 > eleven_multilingual_v2 for English voicemail quality (ElevenLabs)
- VibeVoice on RunPod is 4x cheaper than Cartesia with comparable quality

### What Makes A Great Voicemail
- Sounds like someone talking, not reading
- Has natural pauses (the `...` technique)
- References something specific (listing, days on market, price drops)
- Ends with a soft close, not a hard sell
- 8-15 seconds is the sweet spot
