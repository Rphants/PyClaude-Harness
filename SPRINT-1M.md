# AgentRVM 90-Day Sprint to $1M ARR

## Objective

Get AgentRVM from pre-launch to more than `$83,333` MRR within 90 days.
This plan targets `$101,810` exit MRR (`$1,221,720` ARR) so there is margin for slippage.

AgentRVM is positioned for real estate wholesalers and investors:
"AI voicemail + follow-up that turns cold leads into callbacks."

## Offer And Pricing

All ARR math below uses recurring revenue only. Setup fees and one-off service revenue do not count.

| Tier | Price | Who it is for | Core promise |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Starter | `$299/mo` | Solo wholesalers and small investors | Launch one market, run AI voicemail campaigns, basic reporting |
| Growth | `$799/mo` | Teams with acquisition reps | Multi-campaign routing, CRM integration, shared inbox, better analytics |
| Enterprise | `$2,500/mo` | Larger operators and agencies | Multi-market rollout, custom workflows, priority support, white-glove onboarding |

## Week 1-2: Production Deploy + First Ads

### Goal

Go live, prove people will pay, and get the first 8 customers.

### Must Ship

- Deploy a stable production version with live billing, campaign launch flow, usage tracking, and error alerting.
- Make onboarding self-serve enough that a buyer can sign up, import a list, launch, and see the first callback without manual engineering help.
- Add one clear landing page, one demo video, one ROI calculator, and one checkout path.
- Instrument the funnel end to end: ad click, lead, booked demo, activated trial, paid conversion, first callback.
- Prepare 3 ad angles and 6 creatives for Meta and Google Search.

### Traffic Plan

- Spend `$2,100` over 14 days (`$150/day`).
- Target blended CPL: `<= $30`.
- Expected leads: `70` qualified leads.
- Expected demo or trial starts: `18` (`26%` of leads).
- Expected paid customers: `8` (`44%` of demos, `11%` of leads).

### Revenue Math

- New customers in this phase: `6 Starter + 2 Growth`
- New MRR added: `(6 x 299) + (2 x 799) = $3,392`
- Exit MRR at end of Week 2: `$3,392`
- ARR run-rate at end of Week 2: `$40,704`

### Operating Rules

- Every lead gets contacted in under 5 minutes.
- Ronald does founder-led demos for the first 20 opportunities to learn objections directly.
- If landing-page conversion is below `5%`, rewrite the offer before increasing spend.
- If activation to first campaign launch is below `60%`, fix onboarding before buying more traffic.

## Week 3-4: Optimize CPL, Scale Ads

### Goal

Turn the first signal into a repeatable paid acquisition loop and reach 20 total customers.

### Work

- Kill the bottom half of creatives every 72 hours.
- Narrow targeting to the highest-intent audience segments only.
- Add retargeting for site visitors, abandoned checkouts, and demo no-shows.
- A/B test headline, VSL, CTA, and guarantee on the landing page.
- Automate lead follow-up by SMS, email, and voicemail so no lead sits untouched.
- Capture 3 customer proof points and put them in ads and on-page immediately.

### Traffic Plan

- Spend another `$4,200` over 14 days (`$300/day`).
- Drive blended CPL down to `<= $20`.
- Expected leads: `210`.
- Expected demo or trial starts: `42` (`20%` of leads).
- Expected new paid customers: `12`.

### Revenue Math

- New customers in this phase: `8 Starter + 4 Growth`
- New MRR added this phase: `(8 x 299) + (4 x 799) = $5,588`
- Cumulative customer count: `20`
- Exit MRR at end of Week 4: `$8,980`
- ARR run-rate at end of Week 4: `$107,760`

### Rules For Scaling

- Do not increase spend again unless blended CAC stays under one month of first-month gross profit.
- Do not scale a channel with lead-to-paid below `4%`.
- Push every new buyer for a review, screenshot, or case study inside the first 7 days.

## Month 2: First 50 Paying Customers + Referral Program

### Goal

Cross the first 50 paying customers and make referrals a real acquisition channel.

### Work

- Launch a formal referral offer:
  - Customer referral: one free month or `$300` account credit per paying referral.
  - Partner referral: `20%` recurring commission for 12 months for consultants, VAs, and agencies serving wholesalers.
- Package onboarding into a 24-hour "go live" promise.
- Publish 5 strong case studies with actual callback, appointment, or deal pipeline numbers.
- Add in-app upgrade prompts once an account hits usage limits or adds team members.
- Build a weekly webinar/demo that closes multiple accounts at once.

### Traffic Plan

- Spend `$12,000` during Month 2.
- Target blended CPL: `<= $15`.
- Expected leads from paid traffic: `800`.
- Expected demo or trial starts: `120`.
- Expected paid customers from ads: `24`.
- Expected paid customers from referral and partner channel: `6`.
- Total new customers in Month 2: `30`.

### Revenue Math

- New customers in this phase: `21 Starter + 9 Growth`
- New MRR added this phase: `(21 x 299) + (9 x 799) = $13,470`
- Cumulative customer count: `50`
- Exit MRR at end of Month 2: `$22,450`
- ARR run-rate at end of Month 2: `$269,400`

### Month 2 KPIs

- Referral share of new customers: at least `20%`
- Activation to paid conversion: at least `25%`
- Net revenue retention from upgrades and add-ons: positive every week
- Churn: below `5%` monthly

## Month 3: Scale To 200 Customers + Enterprise Tier

### Goal

Scale hard without breaking onboarding, then use enterprise deals to push past `$1M` ARR.

### Work

- Launch the Enterprise tier with custom onboarding, multi-market support, and SLA-backed support.
- Build a simple outbound list of the top 200 wholesalers, investor teams, and agencies and run founder-led enterprise outreach.
- Roll out a partner motion with agencies, dialer shops, and CRM consultants already selling into the same buyers.
- Turn the best-performing webinar, demo script, and case study into standardized sales assets.
- Tighten onboarding so at least `70%` of new customers launch a campaign in the first 24 hours.

### Traffic Plan

- Spend `$30,000` during Month 3.
- Target blended CPL: `<= $12`.
- Expected leads from paid traffic: `2,500`.
- Expected demo or trial starts: `300`.
- Expected new SMB customers from paid traffic: `120`.
- Expected new SMB customers from referral and partner channels: `20`.
- Expected new Enterprise customers from founder-led outbound and inbound: `10`.
- Total new customers in Month 3: `150`.

### Revenue Math

- New customers in this phase: `115 Starter + 25 Growth + 10 Enterprise`
- New MRR added this phase: `(115 x 299) + (25 x 799) + (10 x 2500) = $79,360`
- Cumulative customer count: `200`
- Exit MRR at end of Month 3: `$101,810`
- ARR run-rate at end of Month 3: `$1,221,720`

### Enterprise Offer

- Price: `$2,500/mo`
- Target buyer: teams running multiple markets, internal acquisitions teams, or agencies managing campaigns for clients
- Promise: faster rollout, custom workflow support, priority response, and better reporting
- Sales motion: founder-led demo, paid pilot, close annual after proving response rates

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not scale broken onboarding.
- Do not hide behind vanity metrics. Leads do not matter if paid conversions are weak.
- Put proof into the funnel as soon as it exists: screenshots, callbacks, booked appointments, and revenue stories.
- Protect speed. Shipping slowly is the main risk in this sprint, not overbuilding.
- If Day 30 is below `$7,000` MRR, tighten the offer and messaging before pushing ad spend harder.

## Bottom Line

AgentRVM gets to `$1M+` ARR in 90 days only if the team does four things fast:
ship production in Week 1, find a paid channel by Week 4, hit 50 customers by Day 60, and use referrals plus enterprise to force ARPU up in Month 3.

This is aggressive. It is still realistic if the product works, onboarding is tight, and proof gets fed back into ads every week.
